What you can say
  • where is the arm right now?
  • rotate joint 1 by 10 degrees (joints 1 to 6: base, shoulder, elbow, wrist 1, wrist 2, wrist 3)
  • now move it back
  • turn the wrist 15 degrees
  • go home (arm straight up)
  • stop, or the Stop button, which does not wait for the model
  1. Base (shoulder pan): turns the whole arm left and right
  2. Shoulder (shoulder lift): raises and lowers the upper arm
  3. Elbow: bends the forearm
  4. Wrist 1: tilts the hand up and down
  5. Wrist 2: swings the hand sideways
  6. Wrist 3: spins the tool; "the wrist" on its own means this one

Shown bent for clarity. Home is the arm pointing straight up. Positive degrees on joint 1 turn it anticlockwise seen from above.

One joint per command, moves are relative to where the arm is now, and the safety layer refuses anything over 30 degrees. Before any motion a card asks you to confirm.
